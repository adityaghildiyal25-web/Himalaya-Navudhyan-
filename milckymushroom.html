Introduction

Discover one of India's important warm-climate edible mushrooms.

What is Milky Mushroom?

Milky Mushroom, scientifically known as Calocybe indica, is an edible mushroom recognized for its white colour, firm texture and attractive appearance.

It grows well in warm and humid conditions, making it particularly suitable for tropical and subtropical regions such as India.

Because of its attractive appearance, nutritional value and commercial potential, Milky Mushroom is becoming increasingly important for mushroom growers.

History of Milky Mushroom

From scientific research to commercial cultivation.

Milky mushroom is a relatively newer cultivated mushroom compared with species such as button and oyster mushrooms.


Research and cultivation development of Milky Mushroom in India became prominent during the 1990s, particularly through agricultural research institutions.


Scientists worked on cultivation methods suitable for India's warm climate. The mushroom gained attention because it can grow under conditions where many other commercial mushrooms face difficulty due to higher temperatures.


Over time, techniques involving spawn production, substrate preparation, casing, humidity management and harvesting were developed and improved.

How to Grow Milky Mushroom

The basic cultivation process from substrate preparation to harvesting.

1

Substrate

Select clean agricultural waste such as suitable cereal straw.

2

Preparation

Cut the straw, add suitable moisture and prepare the growing material.

3

Pasteurization

Treat the substrate to reduce unwanted microorganisms.

4

Spawning

Mix or layer good-quality mushroom spawn with the prepared substrate.

5

Incubation

Keep spawned bags in a clean environment for mycelial growth.

6

Casing

Apply a suitable casing layer after sufficient substrate colonization.

7

Fruiting

Maintain suitable humidity, temperature, ventilation and light.

8

Harvesting

Harvest mature mushrooms carefully while maintaining their freshness and quality.

How to Maintain Milky Mushroom

Proper care is essential for healthy growth and good yield.

✓
Maintain Cleanliness

Keep the cultivation room, tools, containers and working area clean.

✓
Maintain Humidity

Maintain suitable humidity while avoiding excessive water accumulation.

✓
Proper Ventilation

Provide sufficient fresh air while avoiding excessive drying.

✓
Monitor Temperature

Keep temperature within the recommended range for the particular cultivation stage.

✓
Control Contamination

Regularly inspect for unwanted mould, insects and bacterial contamination.

✓
Proper Watering

Use clean water and avoid excessive spraying on fruit bodies.

Packaging for Selling

Proper packaging protects mushrooms and improves their market presentation.

Suitable Packaging

  • Food-grade plastic trays
  • Perforated plastic packaging
  • Food-grade pouches
  • Transparent food containers
  • Packaging with suitable air exchange

Packing Process

  • Select fresh and healthy mushrooms.
  • Remove damaged mushrooms.
  • Clean visible dirt carefully.
  • Sort mushrooms by size and quality.
  • Place suitable quantity in food-grade packaging.
  • Add appropriate ventilation/perforation.
  • Add product label and storage information.
  • Keep the product appropriately chilled during storage.

Label Information

  • Product name
  • Net weight
  • Packing date
  • Best-before/use-by information
  • Storage instructions
  • Producer/Seller information

Why Packaging Matters

  • Protects mushrooms from physical damage.
  • Reduces contamination.
  • Improves product appearance.
  • Makes transportation easier.
  • Builds consumer confidence.
  • Supports organized retail selling.

Conclusion

Milky Mushroom (Calocybe indica) is an important edible mushroom particularly suited to warm and humid climatic conditions.
